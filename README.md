# 🏥 Hospital Executive Health Analytics Report

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes hospital operational data to generate a complete executive-level performance report. It focuses on revenue trends, patient demographics, appointment efficiency, departmental performance, and doctor productivity.

The goal is to transform raw hospital data into actionable business insights for better decision-making.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Hospitals often struggle with:
- Lack of clear visibility into revenue performance
- High appointment cancellation and no-show rates
- Difficulty in identifying top-performing departments and doctors
- Poor understanding of patient demographics and insurance distribution
- Fragmented reporting across multiple dashboards and systems

---

## 💡 Solution

This project solves these issues by:
- Consolidating all hospital data into a structured analytics report
- Identifying key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Analyzing revenue by branch, treatment, and time period
- Evaluating patient behavior and appointment patterns
- Ranking doctor and department performance
- Highlighting operational inefficiencies

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- Total Revenue: 551,249.85
- Insurance Coverage: 100%
- Appointment Cancellation Rate: 25.5%
- Female patients dominate the patient base (65%)
- Central Hospital is the highest revenue generator
- Chemotherapy and MRI are top revenue treatments
- Significant revenue fluctuations observed across months
- High no-show and cancellation rates impact efficiency
